## Extension name
DualSubtitle – Bilingual YouTube Subtitles

## Short description (132文字以内)
See real-time Japanese translation below every English subtitle on YouTube. Perfect for language learners. No account needed.

## Detailed description
DualSubtitle instantly shows a Japanese translation beneath YouTube's English subtitles — so you can follow along in both languages without pausing or switching tabs.

**How it works**
1. Open any YouTube video that has English subtitles
2. Turn on subtitles in YouTube's settings
3. The Japanese translation appears automatically below each subtitle line

**Features**
- Real-time translation powered by Google Translate
- Smart caching: repeated subtitles display instantly with zero delay
- Simple ON/OFF toggle from the extension popup
- Works in fullscreen mode
- No account, no sign-up, no data collected

**Who is it for?**
- Japanese learners studying with English YouTube content
- English speakers who want to understand Japanese alongside English
- Anyone who wants to absorb a language naturally through video
