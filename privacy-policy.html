Privacy Policy – DualSubtitle

Last updated: 2026-05-20

What data we collect

DualSubtitle does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

Subtitle text and translation

When a YouTube subtitle appears, its text is sent to Google Translate solely to produce a translation. This request is made directly from your browser to Google's servers. We do not receive, log, or store the content of any subtitle.

Extension settings

Your ON/OFF preference is saved using Chrome's built-in storage API and may sync across your signed-in Chrome devices. No other data is stored.

Third-party services

Translation is performed by Google Translate. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles translation requests.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL.
